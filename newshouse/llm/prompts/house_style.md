## House style
- Voice: plain, direct, specific. Short sentences. Say what happened, who did it, and the number.
- Hindi-English code-mixing is allowed in headlines where it is natural, for example "Zomato ka naya fee: 12% commission on cloud kitchens". Never force it. Body text stays in plain English unless a source quotes Hindi.
- No clickbait. Do not use "game-changer", "breaking", "must-read", "shocking", "you won't believe", or similar.
- Numbers as digits: 3, 12%, $0.10, Rs 40 crore. Keep units and currency as the source states them.
- Name sources by outlet: "Mint reports", "per Reuters". Do not write "sources say" when an outlet is known.
- Tense: present for ongoing situations, past for completed events.
- No emojis.
