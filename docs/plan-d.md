# News House v2, Phase 3 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The paper becomes responsive: clearer carousels, an "Also today" list with one line per story and a mix of topics, live air-quality and next-match tiles, "Tune tomorrow" chips that steer the next edition, a lessons log for missed stories, and a night palette. Hosting on Vercel with Neon is planned here but waits for Mihir's accounts.

**Architecture:** Each part is a small, separate unit. The carousel and night palette are template, CSS and JS changes. "Also today" moves its rules into selection (`select.py`), with one new column to fold extra top stories. Live tiles are a new pure-fetch module (`newshouse/live.py`) called once per build and refreshed by the page. Tune chips add a generator in `present.py`, a page control, and a next-edition adjustment in `score_and_select`. Lessons are a committed JSON file with a small module and CLI, read by the classify prompt and by the validator.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.12+, SQLAlchemy 2, pydantic 2, Jinja2, httpx; pytest; headless Chrome (dev only).

**Spec:** `docs/design-v2.md`, sections 3.1 (night tokens), 3.5 (slides), 3.8 (live tiles), 3.9 (end card and tune chips), 4.1 (lessons in classify), 4.5 (owed check), 4.7 (lessons ledger), 4.8 and 4.9 (tune feedback), 5, 6, and phase 3 in section 7. Mihir's feedback on the first phase 2 edition (2026-11-03) adds the carousel and "Also today" changes below; where it differs from the spec, his feedback wins.

## Mihir's feedback this plan implements (2026-11-03)

1. "Also today" was 25 lines about the same pollution story. It must show one line per story and a mix of topics.
2. The carousel's dots and counter must sit right below the slides, so a reader can tell it is a carousel at a glance.
3. The boxed previous and next buttons go. In their place: small chevrons drawn on the slide itself, with no box, faded so they don't fight the content, shown only on the side where there is another slide.

## Rulings made in this plan

1. **"Also today" rules (feedback 1).** At most 2 lines per primary topic (`also.per_topic` in `config/edition.yaml`, default 2), and no line for a cluster whose thread already has a printed card or an earlier line. A non-top cluster over the cap is simply left out, so other topics fill the 25 slots. A top cluster over the cap is kept as a folded row, still with its cut reason, so the "printed or explained" rule holds. The page shows folded rows as one line per topic, "+N more <Topic> stories cut by the topic cap", which expands. Similarity by embeddings was measured and rejected: two different Barça stories scored 0.84, higher than the median pair of duplicate pollution reports (0.77).
2. **Carousel controls (feedback 2 and 3).** The dots and the `n / N` counter move into the deck, directly under the slides. The chevrons sit inside the slide at mid-height, 44px tap areas with an 18px glyph in the slide's ink at 55% opacity, no box or background. The left one is hidden on slide 1 and the right one on the last slide. Keyboard arrows and swipe stay. In the expanded post, the chevrons and dots hide, as the whole nav does today.
3. **Photos load eagerly.** `loading="lazy"` is dropped from post photos so the start-of-page broken-image check is reliable in iOS Safari. An edition has one to three photos, so the cost is small.
4. **`newshouse render` refreshes `out/latest.html`** when it renders the newest built edition, as `tick` already does.
5. **Air quality is India's CPCB AQI computed from Open-Meteo's PM2.5 and PM10.** Open-Meteo gives US and European AQI only. The tile says "est." so it is not mistaken for the official CPCB reading.
6. **The fixture tile uses ESPN's public team schedule** (`site.api.espn.com`, Barcelona is team 83). Both sources were checked on 2026-11-03: they need no key and allow cross-origin reads, so the page can refresh them.
7. **Tune chips are generated by a fixed rule.** "Less <the topic with the most posts>", "More <the topic with the best-scored cut story>", "More <the next such topic>", and "Shorter edition", or "Longer edition" when the edition ran under 12 minutes. A tap counts for the next built edition only: a topic weight moves by 0.3 (clamped to -1 to 1), the budget by 120 seconds.
8. **Lesson stubs from "Should have been in" taps are made at the next build,** not by the server, so the page server never writes repo files. Stubs are idempotent by feedback id.
9. **Night palette follows the system setting,** with a `?theme=dark` or `?theme=light` URL override (used by `shot.py --theme`). Mint and sky posts keep their fills, since spec 3.1 says paint colours stay; dark muted text is `#9DB5A6`.
10. **Not in this phase:** spec 3.10's motion list is already met. Selection budgeting with the old reading times stays, because the visual is unknown at selection time and deck stories mostly come from story types already budgeted at 50 seconds. The clustering stage's failure to merge 25 reports of one event is a phase 4 item; this plan fixes what the reader sees.

No shadows, no gradients, no emoji. Tap targets at least 44px. No text under 11px. A post's visual is at most 320px tall at widths of 430 or less.

- Schema changes are additive with defaults through `ensure_columns`; the 30 October and 3 November editions re-render without a rebuild.
- A live-tile fetch failure never fails a build: it logs and renders no tile.
- The 363 existing tests keep passing; a test changes only where this plan changes the behaviour it pins, and the task says which.

## Review Focus

- An edition where every candidate is from one topic: "Also today" holds at most 2 lines of it plus folded top rows, never 25 lines of one story. Test in Task 2 (`test_also_one_topic_flood_is_capped`).
- A one-slide-wide viewport where the reader is on the last slide: the right chevron is hidden, the left one shows, and a tap on the hidden side does nothing. Test in Task 1 (`test_deck_chevrons_and_dots_markup`) plus the JS state check in the same task.
- Open-Meteo or ESPN returns 500, times out, or returns JSON without the expected keys: the build succeeds, `Edition.live` lacks that tile, and the page hides it. Test in Task 3 (`test_live_fetch_failures_are_silent`).
- A tune tap made before today's build and another made after it: only the later one shapes tomorrow's edition, and only once. Test in Task 4 (`test_tune_applies_to_next_edition_only`).
- A "Should have been in" tap on a cut top line, seen by two builds: one lesson stub, not two. Test in Task 5 (`test_lesson_stub_is_idempotent`).

### Task 3: Live tiles

**Files:**
- Create: `newshouse/live.py`
- Modify: `newshouse/models.py` (`Edition.live: dict`, default `{}`), `newshouse/build.py` (a `live` stage after `select`), `newshouse/render_static.py` (`live` in the view), `newshouse/templates/edition.html.j2`, `newshouse/static/edition.css`, `newshouse/static/edition.js`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces in `newshouse/live.py`:
  - `cpcb_aqi(pm25: float | None, pm10: float | None) -> int | None`: the CPCB sub-index for each pollutant by linear interpolation over the CPCB breakpoints (PM2.5: 0-30, 31-60, 61-90, 91-120, 121-250, 250+; PM10: 0-50, 51-100, 101-250, 251-350, 351-430, 430+; AQI bands 0-50, 51-100, 101-200, 201-300, 301-400, 401-500), the higher of the two, capped at 500.
  - `cpcb_band(aqi: int) -> tuple[str, str]`: `(label, dot)` with labels Good, Satisfactory, Moderate, Poor, Very poor, Severe, and dots `good` for Good and Satisfactory, `butter` for Moderate, `tomato` above.
  - `fetch_aqi(client, *, lat: float, lon: float) -> dict | None`: GET `https://air-quality-api.open-meteo.com/v1/air-quality` with `current=pm2_5,pm10` and `timezone=Asia/Kolkata`; returns `{"aqi", "band", "dot", "at"}` or `None`.
  - `fetch_next_match(client, *, team_id: int, team_name: str, now: datetime) -> dict | None`: GET `https://site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/soccer/all/teams/{team_id}/schedule?fixture=true`; the first event dated after `now`; returns `{"text": "Barça v Aston Villa", "when": "Tue 1:30 am", "iso": <utc iso>, "competition": "UEFA Champions League"}` with the time in IST, the configured team written as "Barça" first when it is the home side, "<home> v Barça" otherwise.
  - `fetch_live(client, cfg: EditionConfig, *, now: datetime) -> dict` combining both under keys `aqi` and `match`, omitting a key whose fetch returned `None`. Every exception and non-2xx status is logged at warning and becomes `None`.
- `config/edition.yaml` `live.fixtures` gains `team_id: 83`.
- The page: a row of two white tiles under the masthead, per spec 3.8: "Gurgaon air now", the AQI number, the band with its dot, and "est. from PM2.5/PM10"; "Next match", the fixture text and the IST time. A tile with no data is not rendered; the row is not rendered when both are missing. JS `liveTiles()` refetches both every 30 minutes while the page is visible, using the same URLs (put on the tile as `data-src`), and updates the numbers; a failed refresh leaves the tile as it was.
