Who decides what

Mac · launchdWhen
  • every 2 h, only while awake
  • runs whatever is on main
CodeThe rules
  • dedupe, thresholds, caps, budget
  • validation: citations, 90-char headline
  • what counts as breaking
HaikuThe judgement
  • same event or not
  • how much you'd regret missing it
  • which saga it belongs to
OpusThe words
  • visual, slides, tone from the evidence
  • headline, four depths, why
  • never a fact without a source
MihirThe taste
  • profile.md is the brief
  • reactions retrain the ranker
  • sources, merges, Reddit, Neon
